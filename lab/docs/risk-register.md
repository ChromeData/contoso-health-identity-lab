# Identity risk register (lab scenario)

**Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Scope:** Contoso Health, the
lab's fictional 5,000-user hospital. **Risk acceptor:** CISO.

Ratings are judgement, not measurement. **Likelihood / Impact:** H / M / L.
**Residual** assumes the lab's controls are applied as written (staging
flags on; Conditional Access report-only). No tenant exists, so every
control is design evidence, not operating evidence. Status words: **RAN ·
harness** (local harness, hashed record in `lab/evidence/`), **BUILT**
(offline-tested code), **DESIGNED** (written only).

## How this register is used

- **High residual** (R01, R03, and R09 for the single DC) needs a dated plan
  or a signed acceptance with an expiry. R01 is accepted for the lab today
  (ADR 0005); that acceptance doesn't carry into production.
- **Built is not closed.** A BUILT or RAN · harness control lowers a rating
  only on the assumption it is applied as written. Each has an evidence
  capture in `evidence-capture-plan.md`, and the rating is revisited when
  the capture exists.
- Each risk is reviewed on its date and after any incident, any change to a
  listed control, or any new CI identity.
- Closing a risk needs evidence from the tenant (an evidence pack, a drill
  log, a report), not a code change alone.
